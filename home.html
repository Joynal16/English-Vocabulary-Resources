Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things learners ask most before they get started.

Start Learning
What is English Janala?

English Janala is a beginner-friendly platform that helps you build your English vocabulary through interactive lessons, one level at a time.

Do I need to sign up to start learning?

Just use the Get Started form at the top of the page. Enter your name and password and you can begin right away.

How do I choose a lesson?

Under “Let’s Learn Vocabularies” you will see a button for each lesson level. Tap one and its words appear in the grid below.

Can I search for a specific word?

Yes. Type the word into the Search Vocabulary box and press Search to find it without browsing through the levels.

What can I learn from each word?

Every word shows up as a card. Open its details to see the meaning and more information that helps you remember it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The site adapts to mobile, tablet, laptop and desktop screens, so you can learn wherever you are.
